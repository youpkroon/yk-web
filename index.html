About

From idea to working hardware.

Y Kroon Industrial Development & Operations turns ideas into working products through hands-on engineering, rapid prototyping and practical testing. From one-off workshop builds to manufacturable products, the focus is on making hardware that works — simply, quickly and with production in mind.

Workshop / Prototyping

Made in-house.

Nothing moves faster than making it yourself. That’s why we run our own workshop with manual and CNC mills, a lathe, laser cutting, 3D printing, and a fully equipped electronics bench for soldering, assembly and testing. Parts that would take weeks to source can be made in hours, taking ideas from screen to physical prototype in a single day. It keeps development fast, flexible and fully in our hands — and it’s one of the most satisfying parts of the job.
