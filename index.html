From idea to working hardware.

Workshop / Prototyping

Made in-house.

Nothing moves faster than making it yourself. That's why YK runs its own workshop with manual and CNC mills, a lathe, laser cutting, 3D printing, and a bench for soldering, assembly and testing. With these basic tools, we can make a wide range of prototype parts in many different materials. Parts that would take weeks to source can be made in hours, taking ideas from screen to physical prototype in a single day. It keeps development fast, flexible and fully in our hands, and it's one of the most satisfying parts of the job.
